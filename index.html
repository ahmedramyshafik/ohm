What We Know After Reading This Matrix

  • Voltage (V): Acts as the pressure pushing electrical charges through a circuit. Higher Voltage results in greater Current flow and increased Power consumption.
  • Current (I): Represents the flow rate of electric charge. It is directly proportional to Voltage and inversely proportional to Resistance.
  • Resistance (R): Opposes current flow within the circuit. Increasing Resistance decreases Current and total Power output.
  • Power (P): Measures the total electrical work performed per second ($P = V \times I$), combining force (Voltage) and flow rate (Current).
